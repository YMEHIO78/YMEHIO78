What to do about it

  • Fix the 3 zero-freshness organisations first. Nothing else on this page can be trusted where the input is a year old.
  • Name a second person for Catalyst Center, Cisco IOS XE, Terraform. These have the shortest path to a backup: someone already claims the skill at intermediate level.
  • Prompt a profile update when learning completes. 7 workers have current capability that the system of record does not know about.
  • Retire the skills matrix or make it the source. Maintaining both guarantees they disagree; the matrix is a year stale and uses a rating scale that cannot be compared with Workday's.
  • Repoint the voucher total and re-baseline the FY27 budget against $6,226, not $2,071.

Method and limits

  • Evidence precedence. A manager assessment outranks a self assessment; both outrank the skills matrix. Where sources disagree the stronger evidence wins and the disagreement is logged, not averaged.
  • Depth requires a comparable scale. The matrix's legacy H/M/L ratings are translated onto 1–5 as an approximation, and approximations never establish that someone is deep in a skill — they would have manufactured 4 false experts.
  • A blank is not a zero. An empty matrix cell means nobody filled it in. Only an explicit "no" counts as declared no exposure.
  • Small population. 21 people in headcount. Percentages move roughly 5 points per person; read the counts, not the decimals.
  • Criticality is a judgement maintained in data/reference/critical_skills.csv, not derived from the data. It is the one input here a reader should challenge first.
  • Fiscal shorthand is not a date. Expiries recorded as "Q1 FY28" or "end of FY" are left null rather than guessed, so they cannot be alerted on until someone enters a real date.
